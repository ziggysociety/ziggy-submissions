How shipping works

Shipping is charged separately at checkout — a flat ZIGGY rate of $10 domestic (within your own country) or $20 cross-border (NZ↔AU), charged as a flat figure in the customer's own currency — NZ$10 or NZ$20 in New Zealand, A$10 or A$20 in Australia. It's never baked into your price.

You fund your own courier cost out of that shipping fee, and absorb the difference where your real postage runs above the flat rate — talk to us and we'll look at cheaper carriers with you. If cross-Tasman postage genuinely doesn't work, you can list New Zealand-only or Australia-only instead of raising your price, and we'll hide your products from shoppers in the other market.

Our commission is on your product price only; shipping passes through to you in full. Founding sellers are on 15%; sellers joining later are on 20% — your rate is the one recorded in Schedule A of your Seller Agreement. We don't need a SKU here — it comes through automatically from Syncio.

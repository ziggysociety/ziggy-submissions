How shipping works

Shipping is charged separately at checkout — a flat ZIGGY rate of about $10 (local) or $15 (cross-border NZ↔AU), in the customer's currency. It's not baked into your price.

Our commission is on your product price only; shipping passes through to you in full. Founding sellers are on 15%; sellers joining later are on 20% — your rate is the one recorded in Schedule A of your Seller Agreement. We don't need a SKU here — it comes through automatically from Syncio.

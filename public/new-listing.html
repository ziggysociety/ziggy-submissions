Shipping
How shipping works

Shipping is charged separately at checkout as a flat ZIGGY rate in the customer's own currency — a New Zealand customer is charged in NZ$, an Australian customer in A$. It is never baked into your price. Which rate applies depends on your band, agreed at onboarding and recorded in Schedule A.

Standard — a typical single item under 1kg. Jewellery, accessories, clothing, small leather goods. $10 domestic, $25 cross-Tasman.
Heavy — a typical single item over 1kg. Homewares, ceramics, heavy leather. $20 domestic, $35 cross-Tasman.

Domestic means within your own country; cross-Tasman is New Zealand to Australia, or Australia to New Zealand. Shipping is charged once per brand, per order — several pieces from you in one basket is a single shipping charge, not one per item.

You fund your own courier cost out of that shipping fee, and absorb the difference where your real postage runs above the flat rate. Three things make the flat rate go further: use an economy tracked service rather than express, since customers expect 3–10 working days across the Tasman; open a free business account (NZ Post Small Business Sending, or Australia Post MyPost Business), which prices well below the retail counter; and weigh the finished parcel, because gift boxes, candles and tissue can take a 250g order to 1kg. Please don't add a buffer to your price instead; your ZIGGY price must match your own retail price.

Our commission is on your product price only, never on shipping — 15% for founding sellers, 20% for sellers joining later, as recorded in Schedule A of your Seller Agreement. Always ship tracked, and dispatch within 3 business days (or your stated made-to-order turnaround).

Shipping
How shipping works

Shipping is charged separately at checkout — a flat ZIGGY rate of $10 domestic (within your own country) or $20 cross-border (NZ↔AU), set in NZD and converted for customers shopping in another currency. It's never baked into your price.

You fund your own courier cost out of that shipping fee, and absorb the difference where your real postage runs above the flat rate — talk to us and we'll look at cheaper carriers with you. If cross-Tasman postage genuinely doesn't work, you can list New Zealand-only or Australia-only instead of raising your price.

Our commission is on your product price only; shipping passes through to you in full. Founding sellers are on 15%; sellers joining later are on 20% — your rate is the one recorded in Schedule A of your Seller Agreement. Always ship tracked, and dispatch within 3 business days (or your stated made-to-order turnaround).

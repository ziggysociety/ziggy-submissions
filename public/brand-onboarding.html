Price parity
Your ZIGGY prices must match your own

Every product you list with us must be priced the same as it is on your own website. Please don't raise your ZIGGY price to cover postage or commission — shipping is charged separately at checkout and passes through to you in full. You may round to a neat NZD figure, but not inflate.

ZIGGY Certified
The ZIGGY Five Criteria — tell us how you meet them

1. Independent — founder-led or small-team, no corporate ownership or mass-market retailers.

2. Ethical — genuine care for people, planet or community (sustainable materials, fair production, giving back, certified practices).

3. Quality — well-made products to a genuine craft standard (not necessarily premium).

4. Story — an authentic reason to exist, a clear point of view, something worth championing.

5. NZ or AU based — local-first; for brands based and making in Aotearoa New Zealand or Australia.
